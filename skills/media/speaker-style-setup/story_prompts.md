# Speaker Style Story Prompts

Record or dictate these in one natural take. Do not perform. Do not read like an audiobook. Talk like
you are explaining it to a person you actually work with.

## Core stories

1. Tell me about a thing you shipped recently and why it mattered.
2. Tell me about a bug or failure that annoyed you and what the real lesson was.
3. Explain a feature you like to a teammate at a whiteboard.
4. Explain the same feature to a skeptical exec.
5. Pick something overcomplicated and explain how you would simplify it.
6. Give me a funny or gruff aside you would actually say in a demo.
7. Explain the project to a new hire.
8. Explain the project to someone already deep in it.

## Product demo variants

For one feature, say three versions:

- 20-second hallway version
- 60-second team demo version
- 2-minute deep dive version

## Vocabulary calibration

Answer quickly:

- Words you say all the time.
- Words you hate in product demos.
- Phrases that sound like you.
- Phrases that absolutely do not sound like you.
- How formal should this get for execs?
- How casual can it be for teammates?
